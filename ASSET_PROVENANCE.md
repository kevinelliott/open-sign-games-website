# Restaurant artwork provenance

The four restaurant environment illustrations were generated with the built-in
OpenAI ImageGen tool on 2026-08-01, then resized to 1440 pixels wide and encoded
as local WebP assets at quality 84. The generated originals remain outside the
repository; the optimized project assets are authoritative for this site.

All prompts requested premium high-detail 16-bit pixel art, a wide cinematic
environment, crisp hard pixel clusters, no text, no logos, no user interface,
no watermark, and no photorealism.

## Dos Esposas

A richly authored after-hours cantina kitchen organized around visible choose,
combine, cook, and inspect stations, with hand-painted tile, copper cookware,
chiles, herbs, clay pots, and a central preparation counter. Palette: ink black,
terracotta coral, ember orange, muted turquoise, and warm cream. The prompt
explicitly excluded stereotype costumes and novelty clichés.

## Samurai Sushi

A quiet moonlit sushi counter arranged into precise rice, knife, garnish,
plating, and service zones. Palette: ink black, deep indigo, moon blue, rice
ivory, and restrained vermilion. The prompt explicitly excluded cyberpunk,
stereotype costumes, and novelty clichés.

## The Tender Baron

A symmetrical storybook steakhouse hearth with distinct coal zones, resting
racks, carving block, brass trim, and the dining room beyond. Palette: ink
black, oxblood burgundy, aged brass, ember orange, and bone white. The prompt
excluded generic luxury-hotel imagery and posed characters.

## Fry Signal

A focused late-night fried-chicken kitchen with fryer bank, three illuminated
routing lanes, batch shelf, packing rail, signal lamps, and dispatch window.
Palette: ink black, forest green, signal green, amber, and fried-gold. The prompt
excluded generic fast-food branding and mascots.
